# Airborne 0.3.15

- The trend map has a toolbar with a Layers popover for the fill and markers, and a floating legend you can open or tuck away.
- Each marker in the Layers popover says where it reaches: "Local + state" for wastewater and ED visits, "State" for admissions and positivity.
- Playback steps through the scan's weeks with blended fills, per-week badges, and CDC's weekly ED labels and regional flu positivity for past weeks.
- Up close, local areas are outlined, named and filled with their own reading, and a local area's popover shows its values beside its state's.
- Badges for local areas, states and HHS regions look distinct, and their arrows tilt by how fast each metric is changing.
- A Near me zoom frames the map around your place.
