# Airborne 0.3.26

- Consolidates map controls so illness, Simple/Analyst mode, and camera view each have one place. Simple uses one Measure menu; Analyst uses a Layers inspector on macOS and a sheet on iOS.
- Makes Simple a map of area colors with details on click or tap. Analyst retains the configurable signal markers and saved comparisons.
- Keeps camera framing separate from reporting geography, with Near me range under View. Uses native controls and Liquid Glass on supported systems.
