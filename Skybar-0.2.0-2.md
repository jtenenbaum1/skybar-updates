# Skybar 0.2.0

- Skybar now updates itself. It checks for new versions every hour, shows
  them at the bottom of the panel, and "Check for Updates…" is in the
  actions menu and in Settings.
- The Illness tab: CDC wastewater, hospital admission, and emergency
  department data for your county and state, with the forecast hubs'
  outlook for COVID, flu, and RSV.
- A menu bar cue for illness levels with a threshold of your choosing.
- The app icon.
