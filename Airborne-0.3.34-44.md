# Airborne 0.3.34

- The Briefing now opens with short stories: the biggest change gets a headline and a few sentences on what moved from last week, how the illness's other local signals are doing, and how the country compares. Up to two more rising illnesses get smaller stories.
- Each story links to a new page with that illness's local charts — ED visits, test positivity, hospital admissions and wastewater — each with the national figure alongside.
- Regional lab data now reads "your region" in headlines, with the HHS region named in the story.
- On iPhone, illness pages show their title once, in the navigation bar.
