# Skybar 0.2.4

- Browse a catalog of 13 CDC surveillance illnesses in the Illness window. Choose which illnesses appear in the dropdown and menu bar cue.
- Compare available local and national illness data in the window, and choose the scope used by the dropdown and menu bar cue in Settings.
- Read clearer illness pages and charts with more visible dates, units, source details, and forecast uncertainty where available. Overview cards now use a subtle tint to distinguish activity levels.
- Sort the illness sidebar by family, name, or highest activity within each family.

Coverage and reporting dates vary by illness, source, and geography. Some national or local signals may be unavailable; wastewater detections do not establish a diagnosed human case, and activity levels do not predict individual risk.
