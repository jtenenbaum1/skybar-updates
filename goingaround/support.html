Help for the Going Around iPhone app

Going Around (formerly Airborne) shows public respiratory and other illness surveillance data from CDC and its partners for your area and the United States: wastewater virus levels, emergency department visits, hospital admissions, laboratory test positivity and reported cases. Trends are descriptive summaries of the reported data.

Contact

Email airborneapp@icloud.com with questions, problems or feedback. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and the Going Around version (Settings → About in the app).

Common questions

  • The app shows national data instead of my area. Going Around needs location access to find your state and county. Turn it on in the iPhone Settings app under Going Around → Location (“While Using the App”). Approximate location is enough.
  • The numbers are a week or more old. Public health surveillance data are published on a delay and can be revised. Each screen shows the week the data run through.
  • Some data are missing for my county. Not every program reports every county, so some measures are shown for your state, HHS region or the nation instead.

Important

Going Around is for general information only. It is not medical advice and does not diagnose, treat or predict illness for any person. Data come from public health surveillance, can be delayed or revised, and may not reflect conditions where you are. For medical concerns, contact a health care professional.

Going Around is an independent app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by CDC or Carnegie Mellon University.

Privacy

Going Around has no accounts and collects no data. Read the privacy policy.
