# Airborne 0.3.16

- Airborne is now a full Mac app: it opens its window at launch and stays in the Dock and Command-Tab. Closing its window keeps the menu bar available.
- Local map pills, outlines, and fills now follow each playback week. Pills stay in place and show a muted dash when a week has no reading.
- Past-week local popovers show that week’s readings alongside current values, and markers fade when returning to the live map.
