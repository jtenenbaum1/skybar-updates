# Going Around 0.3.62

- New: illness alerts for Flu, COVID and RSV. Turn them on in Settings › Illness alerts.
  - **Level goes up:** a notification when one of them moves up a level near you, for example Moderate to High.
  - **Level drops back down:** when a level falls back from Moderate or higher.
  - **Forecast warning:** when CDC's forecast says your state will move up a level next week.
  - **Monday summary:** all three levels at 8 AM every Monday.
- At most 2 alerts a week, including the Monday summary. Alerts about High or Very high levels always come through.
- "Near you" is the same reading the app shows: your county's wastewater sites when they reported this week, otherwise your state.
- Clicking an alert opens that illness in the main window.
