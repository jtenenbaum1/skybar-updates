# Airborne 0.3.35

- The weekly timeline is now a row of week segments: click a week to jump to it, or press play. On the Mac, arrow keys step a week at a time when the timeline has focus.
- In Analyst on the Mac, a split-view button in the toolbar switches between one map and two; Linked cameras stays in the header while split.
- The iPhone map is redesigned: the map fills the screen, with Simple | Analyst at the top left, the illness and measure in a centred chip above its colour scale, search at the top right, split view and your location on the right edge, and the week timeline at the bottom.
