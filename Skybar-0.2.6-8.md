# Skybar 0.2.6

- Opt in to home weather alerts in Settings after choosing your home from the current location or a city/ZIP search. Skybar checks Apple Weather for that location about every 15 minutes while the app runs, even if you travel or use Tomorrow.io for the forecast.
- See active home alerts in the menu bar, dropdown, and Weather window. Multiple alerts appear in severity order. Lower-severity alerts use a compact ribbon; severe and extreme alerts use a larger card. Each alert shows its issuing agency and opens the full details. Watch wording appears only when the issuer calls the alert a watch.
- Receive a macOS notification for each new active home alert. Turning alerts off hides the cues and removes Skybar's delivered alert notifications. A brief network failure no longer hides an unexpired warning.
- This update also includes the weather browsing, provider choice, and forecast artwork improvements prepared after the last public feed update.

Alert coverage depends on Apple Weather and the issuing agency. Polling while Skybar runs is not a substitute for an immediate emergency warning service.
