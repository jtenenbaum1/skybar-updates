# Going Around 0.3.52

- One switch at the top of the sidebar now moves between Briefing, Map and Weather. The separate Weather / Illness toggle is gone.
- Going Around asks for your location once, instead of again at every weather refresh while you haven't answered.
- With CDC's flu activity (ILI) colors on the map, the map now uses a light style so place names stay readable.
- New map buttons: show the whole US, or zoom to the area around your location.
