# Going Around 0.3.38

The map now uses distinct color bands for ED visits, test positivity, and hospital admissions, making regional differences easier to see on Mac and iPhone.

- ED visits use cream, gold, peach, rust, and burgundy for the percentage ranges below 0.5%, 0.5–1%, 1–2%, 2–4%, and 4% or more.
- Positivity uses aqua, turquoise, blue, cobalt, and indigo for the percentage ranges below 2%, 2–5%, 5–10%, 10–20%, and 20% or more.
- Admissions keeps its existing Very low through Very high categories, with distinct lavender, periwinkle, violet, magenta, and plum colors.
- Map legends show each band and its range or category. Historical playback uses the band for the displayed reporting week.

Percentage bands are fixed display ranges, not clinical severity categories. Selecting an area still shows the exact reported values and source evidence. Wastewater colors and missing-report indicators retain their existing meanings.
