# Airborne 0.3.8

- New: Trends. The top of Illness › Overview now shows where flu, COVID-19 and RSV are rising, near you first and then elsewhere in the US. It uses CDC wastewater from every sampling site and CDC emergency-department trend labels, and each card has a chart.
- New: Trend map. Illness › Map colors each state by its current wastewater level and marks local areas where wastewater is rising. Switch between flu, COVID-19 and RSV, and between your region and the whole US.
- The menu bar's Illness tab shows one line when something is rising near you. Click it to open the Trends page, or turn it off in Settings.
- Trend data refreshes every few hours, says when a CDC source didn't load, and has a Retry button.
