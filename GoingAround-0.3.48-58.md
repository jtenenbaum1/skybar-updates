# Going Around 0.3.48

- Briefing and Map are now a large switch at the top of the sidebar, with an icon over each name, so they stand out from the illness list. The one you're on is filled in blue.
- In Simple, the map's Local areas now show every local area CDC reports, across the country, not only the ones near where you last zoomed in. Their outlines are thinner and lighter, so they don't crowd the colors.

The web app has the same sidebar switch, and its map loads right away even while it refreshes CDC data.
