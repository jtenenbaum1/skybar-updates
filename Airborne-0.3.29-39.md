# Airborne 0.3.29

- Adds laboratory test positivity to the Briefing's flu, COVID-19 and RSV cards, with its own value, direction, week, place and history chart.
- Flu positivity comes from CDC FluView clinical laboratories (via Delphi Epidata); COVID-19 and RSV come from CDC NREVSS. When a state has no usable lab data, the card uses its HHS region.
- A significant rise or fall in test positivity can now lead the Briefing headline.
