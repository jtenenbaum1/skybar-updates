# Airborne 0.3.30

- The Briefing headline now names the largest significant change across your watched illnesses, not just the first one in your list, and a short "Also rising" line names up to two more.
- Test positivity counts as significant after two weekly rises (or falls) of at least 0.15 points in a row, or a 15% and 0.15-point shift in the two-week average.
- Positivity arrows on the map, Briefing cards and illness pages now show any week-over-week change; small changes read to two decimals.
- Removes the "Each illness below retains its own measure and reporting week" line. The map reloads its data once after updating.
